# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard Project

## 🔍 Project Overview

This project analyzes HR data of **50,000 employees** to understand workforce trends, employee attrition, salary patterns, and department-level performance. The goal is to help HR teams make data-driven decisions to reduce attrition and improve employee retention.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The organization is facing a high attrition rate of **50.21%**, with 25,105 employees leaving the company. This project digs deep into the data to find the key factors driving attrition — including department, distance from home, business travel, salary, and promotion history.

## 📈 Key Insights

- 📌 **Overall Attrition Rate: 50.21%** — 25,105 out of 50,000 employees left
- 👩 **Female Attrition: 12,560** | 👨 **Male Attrition: 12,545**
- 🏢 **R&D department** had the highest attrition count at **16.97%**
- 💰 **Sales department** had the lowest average monthly income at ₹26,065
- 🚗 Employees living **very far** from the office had the highest attrition — **49.86%**
- ✈️ Employees who **travel frequently** showed higher attrition rates
- 📅 **41.44%** of employees who left were promoted within the last 0–10 years
- 💼 **Single employees aged 18–30** had the highest attrition count

## 💡 Recommendations

- Focus retention efforts on **R&D and Sales departments** with highest attrition
- Offer **remote work or transport support** for employees living far from office
- Review **compensation structure** in departments with high attrition and low salary
- Reduce **frequent business travel** requirements or provide better travel benefits
- Introduce better **promotion policies** to retain early-career employees
